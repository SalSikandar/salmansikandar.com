I keep large computers running for scientists.

Hi, I'm Salman. I lead platform engineering at the University of Chicago's Center for Translational Data Science: Linux, Kubernetes, GPU clusters, and the people who need them to work on a Monday morning. I live west of Chicago with my wife and two kids.

Uptime: 12 years Location: Naperville, IL Load: 1 platform, 2 kids Sleep: degraded (newborn)

The rack on the left is this site's table of contents. Each unit is a part of my life, labelled the way I'd label real hardware. Click one and its locate light comes on, like pressing the identify button on a server so you can find it in a dark room.

What I actually do all day: own a production platform that serves controlled-access cancer-genomics data to researchers worldwide, run the Slurm cluster that crunches it, lead incident response when it breaks, and review the designs of the engineers who build on it. In the last two years, most of the interesting problems have been GPUs: turning hand-allocated machines into a shared Kubernetes AI platform with a self-hosted model gateway, inside a strict security boundary, without outside consultants.

U34–U37Work · six bays

Twelve years of running other people's infrastructure.

2020 – now

Principal Platform Engineer, leading the platform function

University of Chicago · Center for Translational Data Science · Chicago, remote

Platform engineering for the NCI Genomic Data Commons, an eight-petabyte-plus cancer-genomics platform used by tens of thousands of researchers. I own architecture, releases, and incident command, and I lead the platform engineers day to day. Also under my name: a Slurm cluster that bursts to AWS Spot, CI/CD across hundreds of repositories, the observability stack, and the enterprise vendor contracts that come with all of that.

The proudest piece is the GPU cluster rebuild. Hand-allocated V100s became a zero-trust, FIPS-hardened Kubernetes AI platform spanning three GPU generations, with a self-hosted model gateway in front of it. I set the architecture and reviewed every design; the team built it. Then we gave every engineer a self-hosted coding assistant on top of it, with no data leaving the center.

2018 – 2020

Lead Pre-sales & Systems Engineer

Motorola Solutions · Muscat, Oman

Lead engineer on a $250M+ public-safety program for a key Middle East customer, and business development across the Gulf. Redesigned a national police operations-centre proposal from a default IBM stack to an open-source architecture at roughly a fifth of the cost. It was selected, came in $4M under budget, and I led the country-wide rollout: a 400-VM server farm, petabyte-scale storage, nationwide IP networks over MPLS and microwave, and the 9999 emergency telephony system.
