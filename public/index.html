U34–U37Work · five bays

Building platforms. Leading the teams behind them.

2020 – now

Principal Platform Engineer

University of Chicago · Center for Translational Data Science · Chicago, remote

I lead platform engineering for the NCI Genomic Data Commons, a cancer-genomics platform providing researchers worldwide with access to open and controlled-access data. I own platform architecture, releases, and incident response, and lead the engineering team day to day. My responsibilities also include a Slurm cluster that bursts to AWS Spot, CI/CD across hundreds of repositories, observability, and enterprise vendor contracts.

One project I'm especially proud of is rebuilding our GPU infrastructure. We moved from manually allocated V100 and H100 GPUs to a shared, zero-trust, FIPS-hardened Kubernetes AI platform with a self-hosted model gateway. I defined the architecture and reviewed the designs; the team built the platform. We then made self-hosted coding assistants available to every engineer, with inference and code kept within the center's infrastructure.

2018 – 2020

Lead Pre-sales & Systems Engineer

Motorola Solutions · Muscat, Oman

Lead pre-sales engineer on a $250M+ public-safety program for a key Middle East customer, and business development across the Gulf.

Separately, I redesigned a multi-million-dollar national police operations-centre proposal, replacing the default end-to-end IBM stack with an open-source architecture at roughly a fifth of the cost. My proposed architecture was selected over the IBM alternative. The project finished $4M under budget, and I led the nationwide rollout: a 400-VM server farm, petabyte-scale storage, nationwide IP networks over MPLS and microwave, and the 9999 emergency telephony system.
